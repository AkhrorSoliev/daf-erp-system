Soft pastel category card — a big rounded title with a trailing value box. Used for vocabulary buckets (Learning / New / All words) and unit sections (Video / Vocabulary / Homework).

```jsx
<CategoryCard tone="sky"  title="O‘rganilayotgan so‘zlar" value="0" onClick={open} />
<CategoryCard tone="pink" title="Yangi so‘zlar" value="0" />
<CategoryCard tone="mint" title="Vocabulary" value="0%" />
```

Tones: sky | pink | sand | grape | mint | peach | coral | teal.
