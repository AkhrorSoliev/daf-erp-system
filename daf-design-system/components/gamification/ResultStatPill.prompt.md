Stat capsule for the battle-result ("Jang natijasi") cards — a colored coin + number.

```jsx
<ResultStatPill kind="correct" icon={<i className="ph-bold ph-check"/>} value="10" filled />
<ResultStatPill kind="time" icon={<i className="ph-fill ph-clock"/>} value="1m 43s" />
```

`filled` switches to the translucent-on-gradient treatment used on the winner card.
